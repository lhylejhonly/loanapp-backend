# ElevateFunds Short User Guide

This guide explains how the system works in simple step-by-step form.

## 1. Borrower Guide

### A. Create an account
1. Open the app.
2. Tap `Create an account`.
3. Enter your username, full name, email, phone number, and password.
4. Use a strong password with at least 8 characters, 1 number, and 1 special character.
5. Enter the email verification code sent to your email.
6. After verification, log in to your account.

### B. Complete your account setup
1. Open `Settings`.
2. In `Contact & GCash profile`, enter your phone number and GCash details.
3. Save your contact and payout information.

### C. Complete the Eligibility Check
1. Stay in `Settings`.
2. Go to the `Eligibility Check` section.
3. Choose your employment status.
4. Enter your monthly income and other required financial details.
5. Upload the required real documents:
   Government ID
   Supporting document such as Student ID, Proof of Income, or Proof of Revenue
6. Tap `Save & Check Eligibility`.
7. Wait for the loan officer to verify your uploaded documents.
8. Once your status becomes `QUALIFIED`, you can submit a loan application.

### D. Apply for a loan
1. Open `Home` or `Loan Programs`.
2. Select the loan program you want.
3. Fill in the loan application form.
4. Choose the amount and repayment term.
5. Confirm your GCash payout details.
6. Read and accept the Terms and Conditions.
7. Tap `Submit`.

Note:
If the button says `Continue to Eligibility Check`, you must finish verification first.

### E. Track your application
1. Open `Stage` to see your loan progress.
2. Check whether your loan is:
   Submitted
   Under Review
   Approved or Rejected
   Released
   In Repayment
3. Open `Notifications` for updates.
4. Open `History` to view your past applications.

### F. After approval
1. Wait for the officer to release the loan.
2. Check your GCash or selected payout account.
3. Open `Repayment Schedule` to view due dates.
4. Make payments based on the schedule provided by the system.

### G. If you need help
1. Open `Settings`.
2. Tap `Support Center`.
3. Use the listed email, phone, or office contact details.

## 2. Loan Officer Guide

### A. Review borrower applications
1. Log in using an officer account.
2. Open `Loan Applications`.
3. Review pending applications.
4. Open the application you want to check.
5. Review borrower details, requested amount, repayment terms, and uploaded documents.

### B. Verify borrower documents
1. Open the borrower record or the application details.
2. Check if the uploaded ID and supporting documents are valid and readable.
3. Mark documents as verified if they are acceptable.
4. If documents are not correct, reject them based on your review process.

### C. Approve or reject a loan
1. Enter the correct interest rate if approval is allowed.
2. Tap `Approve` to approve the loan.
3. If the application should not continue, enter a rejection reason.
4. Tap `Reject`.

### D. Manage borrower accounts
1. Open `Borrower Accounts`.
2. View borrower status, verification status, and balances.
3. Activate or deactivate borrower access when needed.
4. Open borrower documents again if re-checking is needed.

### E. Record releases and payments
1. Open `Payments & Releases`.
2. Record the disbursement reference when a loan is released.
3. Record borrower repayments using the payment form.
4. Check recent payments to confirm that transactions were saved.

## 3. Admin Guide

### A. Review loans
1. Log in using an admin account.
2. Open `Loan Review Center`.
3. Review pending borrower applications.
4. Approve or reject applications when required.

### B. Manage user accounts
1. Open `User Management`.
2. Review borrower, officer, and admin accounts.
3. Activate or deactivate user access as needed.
4. If you are the super admin, create new admin accounts from this screen.

### C. Manage loan products
1. Open `Loan Types`.
2. Add a new loan type when needed.
3. Set the minimum amount, maximum amount, interest rate, and payment terms.
4. Select the required documents for that loan type.
5. Activate or deactivate loan products based on business rules.

### D. View reports
1. Open `Reports`.
2. Review:
   Approved principal
   Collected payments
   Collection rate
   Loan status breakdown
   Monthly payment trend
   Top borrowers

## 4. Important System Rules

1. Borrowers cannot submit a loan application until they pass the strict Eligibility Check.
2. Uploaded IDs and supporting documents must be verified by a loan officer.
3. Borrower payout details are saved in the account for future applications.
4. Officers handle document verification, loan decisions, disbursements, and payment recording.
5. Admins manage users, loan products, and reports.

## 5. Quick Summary

1. Register or log in.
2. Complete `Settings`.
3. Pass the `Eligibility Check`.
4. Apply for a loan.
5. Wait for officer review.
6. Track status in `Stage`.
7. Receive funds if approved.
8. Repay based on the schedule.
